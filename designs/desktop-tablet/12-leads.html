A · Leads — Desktop 1440 (list + detail, one screen)

Decisions

  • Split view. Mobile is a list that pushes to /leads/lead/[id]. On desktop the list stays at 380 on the left and the lead opens on the right — a seller working through eight enquiries stops paying a back-navigation each time. The detail route still exists and still deep-links.
  • The lead's answers stay verbatim — Wants, Contact by, Best time, Number — because those four lines are the entire product of a lead. Nothing is summarised away.
  • The subject sits above the answers: which listing or project this lead is about, with a link through. On mobile that context is a header; here it is a card, so the seller never answers about the wrong property.
  • Status is a chip row, not a hidden filter: Received · Contacted · Converted · Archived, plus the app's own "Only unopened" toggle and its two sorts (Most new leads · Newest activity).
  • Visits and Proposals are the same screen with a different subject: a visit leads with its date block and Overdue state, a proposal with its requirement and reply status.
