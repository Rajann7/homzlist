A · Saved — Desktop 1440 (collections as a carousel, saved items below)

Decisions

  • Collections lead as a carousel. They are the app's own grouping and the reason Saved is more than a list; on desktop they sit across the top, and picking one filters the rail below.
  • The saved items themselves stay carousels per collection — the owner rule holds here too, and it reads better than a wall of identical cards.
  • Sold / rented items keep their overlay. The app stamps RENTED on a saved item that has gone; that stays, because a saved list that quietly hides things is worse than one that tells you.
  • Activity and Saved searches share the screen as tabs at desktop width, since both are short lists a user checks together. The routes are unchanged — the tabs are links, not a merge.
  • Saved searches carry their match count and the app's own "No matches yet" state, with a one-click path back into results.
