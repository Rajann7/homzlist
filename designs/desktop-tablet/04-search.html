04 · Search
Real tabs (All · Properties · Projects · Brokers & Builders · Areas), real sort (Latest · Nearby · Price ↑ · Price ↓) and the real filter sections from FilterSheet: Looking to · Property type · Budget · Location · Amenities · More. No maps anywhere, as in the app.

A · Search results — Desktop 1440 (filters as a rail, results as rows)

Decisions

  • The filter sheet becomes a rail. On mobile, filters live in a bottom sheet because there is no room; on desktop that sheet is a modal standing between the user and the thing they came for. At ≥1200 the same sections — Looking to, Property type, Budget, Location, Amenities, More — sit open in a 280px rail, and every change re-runs the search live. The "Filters" button disappears because there is nothing left for it to open. Below 1200 the button and the sheet come back, unchanged.
  • Results are rows, not a grid. A wide row shows what a grid card cannot: the full title, the area, all the facts, the poster and the actions on one line. This is also the honest reading of "no grids" — the page scrolls one list, exactly like the mobile feed, just wider.
  • The tabs stay the app's five and keep their server-computed badge counts. The cascade sections under the All tab ("Also in Adajan", "Projects near you") become carousels.
  • No map. The app has none, so neither does this: the space a portal would give a map goes to the rail and to wider rows.
  • The search field itself moves into a sticky sub-bar under the header, so scrolling a long result list never loses the query.

B · Search results — Tablet 1024 (filters return to a sheet, opened by the button)

C · Search home — Desktop 1440 (the entry screen: recent, popular areas, browse by type)

Search home, and the area / landing pages

  • Mobile's Search screen is a field plus recent searches. On desktop the field is already in the header and the hero, so this screen earns its keep by being a browse index: recent searches, popular areas as a carousel, and browse-by-type from the app's own type codes (Flat, Bungalow, Tenement, Farmhouse, Office, Shop, Showroom, Godown, Plots, PG).
  • /area/[slug] and /[landing] use the same composition with the area's own title, count line and description — one design, three routes.
  • /search/coming-soon keeps its message centred with the city picker beneath, no rail.
