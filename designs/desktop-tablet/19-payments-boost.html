A · Payments — Desktop 1440 (filter rail + rows)

Decisions

  • Payments is a working list, so it is rows with a filter rail — the same treatment as My Listings. Each row carries the amount, what it paid for, the method, the status and the invoice link, which is exactly the set the app already shows behind its "More options" sheet.
  • Failed payments keep "Check status", because a payment that failed at the gateway but succeeded at the bank is the one case a user genuinely needs to re-query.
  • Boost status leads with what is running. Active boosts show their days-left bar; pending ones keep the app's own "Under review" and "Boosts start after admin approval" line, which is the single most important expectation to set on a paid feature.
  • Buying a boost is options + sticky summary: duration, where to show (This area only / All India — the app's own two choices), and the price that follows. Razorpay's own window is flagged in the same blue strip as checkout.
  • Empty states keep the app's words: "No payments yet", "No active boosts right now.", "No past boosts yet.", "Nothing waiting for approval."
