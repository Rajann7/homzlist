The rules this screen follows

  • Header = the current project's header. Logo + city chip on the left, the primary nav centred (Buy · Rent · Projects · Commercial · Plots · PG · Requirements — the app's own type categories from lib/listings/type-icon.ts), and on the right Dashboard, bell, saved, avatar and the accent Post Property. Under 1080 the nav collapses into the hamburger, exactly as that header does.
  • Carousels everywhere, never a grid. Top picks, newly-added, developers, brokers, featured and news are all rails: 4 cards at 1440, 5 at 1600, 2 at tablet, with prev/next arrows. Stories are a rail too. Nothing on this page is a card grid.
  • The search becomes the hero. On mobile, Search is a screen behind a nav icon; on desktop that is a wasted front door, so the panel leads — Buy/Rent/Projects segment, category, query, Search, and the quick filter chips underneath.
  • Mobile order is untouched: stories → top picks → newly-added → developers → brokers → featured → sell → news → footer.
  • The sell block becomes a band with proof — same server-owned copy ("Have a property to sell?" / "Post it free and reach buyers in Surat"), plus three real counts, which is what stops a desktop CTA from reading as an empty strip.
