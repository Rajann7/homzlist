Decisions

  • Split view again, for the same reason as Leads: a broker scanning twenty-four buyer requirements decides in seconds and should never pay a page load per look. The /requirements/[id] route still works on its own.
  • "I Have a Property" is the panel's primary action. That is the app's own label and the whole commercial point of the screen, so it does not become an icon on a row — it sits full-width in the detail panel, where the requirement it answers is visible.
  • The entitlement wall keeps its place. A seller without the requirement plan sees the app's own locked state with "Instant match alerts for your areas" and "Direct chat after acceptance" — this is the upsell, and hiding it on desktop would remove the reason to buy.
  • Posting a requirement is form + live preview, same as posting a listing, so the buyer sees the card sellers will answer.
  • My requirements and My proposals are the same list with their own status chips.
