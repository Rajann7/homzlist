A · Settings — Desktop 1440 (section nav + panel)

Decisions

  • Settings stops being a stack of screens. On mobile every section is its own route reached by tapping a row. On desktop the sections become a 260px nav and the content shows in place — the routes still exist and still deep-link, so /settings/privacy opens with Privacy selected.
  • The rows keep their exact labels and their exact grouping, including "You can change this per listing" under the number-visibility toggle, which is the one place a user could otherwise misread a global switch.
  • Danger zone stays last and stays red-bordered, with the app's own two actions separated — deactivate is reversible, delete is not, and they must never look like the same button.
  • Login activity gets a real device list with the app's own device labels (Chrome · Safari · Firefox · Android · Linux · Unknown device) and its single action, "Log out this device?".
  • Legal pages, Account status and Blocked users stay links out — they are their own routes and their own screens.
