07 · Story viewer
The one screen that must NOT get a sidebar or a wider layout. A 9:16 story is a phone shape; on desktop it keeps that shape and the page goes dark around it.

A · Desktop 1440 — centred player, previous/next posters either side

Why this, and not a full-screen stretch

  • The story keeps its aspect. 9:16 stretched to 1440×900 either letterboxes into black bars or crops the poster's own framing. So the player stays 400×710 and the page dims around it — the treatment every desktop user already knows from stories elsewhere.
  • The dead space earns its keep. The previous and next posters sit either side at 42% opacity, so the viewer can see whose story is coming and click straight to it. That is a desktop-only affordance; on mobile the same move is a swipe.
  • Keyboard. ← → move between posters, Space pauses, Esc closes. A mouse user has no thumb, so without this the viewer is a click-only experience.
  • The listing CTA stays. The story's attached property card keeps its place at the bottom of the player with the same "View listing" action — that link is the entire commercial point of stories, and it must not be a hover-only affordance.
  • Closing returns to the page underneath, scroll position intact, because the viewer mounts over the feed rather than replacing it. The /story/[posterId] route still works standalone for a shared link; it just renders the same player on a dark page.
