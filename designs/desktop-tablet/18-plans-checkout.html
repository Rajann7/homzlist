A · Plans — Desktop 1440 (a carousel of plans, current plan in the rail)

Decisions

  • Plans ride a carousel, per the owner rule — three visible at 1440, two at tablet, and the currently-active plan is pinned in the right rail so the seller compares against what they already have instead of guessing.
  • The plan wall keeps its exact sentence. "Buy a plan to post your listing" (and the project / requirement variants) is what the app says when a slot runs out, and it stays word for word — this is a commercial screen and rewriting it would change the product.
  • Checkout is content + sticky order summary. Coupon, GSTIN and the method choice on the left; the amount, GST and total pinned on the right, so the number is never scrolled off while a buyer thinks.
  • Razorpay opens in its own window — and the page says so. The blue strip under the pay button tells the buyer a payment window will open and that this page will keep waiting. Without that line, a popup-blocked or manually-closed window looks like a failed payment. The app's "Still processing" and "Payment confirmed" states are both kept.
  • My plan gets a usage bar with the real counter and the app's own options — Top up · Download invoices · Payment history · Expiry reminders on/off · Contact support.
