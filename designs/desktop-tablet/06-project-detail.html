A · Buyer view — Desktop 1440

Decisions

  • Units are a carousel of unit cards, not a table. The mobile screen shows tappable unit cards with Built-up area, Carpet area, Price from and Units available; a desktop table would have been a different component with different data, and the owner rule rules out grids. So the same cards ride a rail — three at a time at 1440, two at tablet.
  • The enquire panel is sticky (360) and carries the price band, the RERA chip and the builder, because on a project page the decision is "talk to this builder", and it must be reachable from the Amenities section as easily as from the top.
  • RERA is never decoration. The chip reads RERA Approved or RERA Exempt exactly as the server says, and the registration number sits in Approvals & documents where the app puts it.
  • Loans keep their own block. "Home loan approved by" with the bank chips — it is a real section in the app and it is a real reason buyers pick a project, so it does not get folded away.
  • Sold-out units keep the disabled "Sold out" state rather than being hidden, as in the app.
