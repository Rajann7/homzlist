Decisions

  • Rows, not cards. This is the seller's working screen: they need status, views, saves, leads and the actions visible at once. A row gives all of it on one line; a card hides half of it behind a menu. (The public feed keeps its carousels — this is the console.)
  • The options sheet becomes inline actions. Mobile hides Edit / Boost / Hide / Mark as sold / Delete behind a "Listing options" sheet. On desktop the two common ones (Edit, Boost) sit on the row and the destructive ones stay in a menu — still with the app's own confirm dialogs ("Mark as sold?", "Hide this listing?").
  • Status filters become a rail: Live · Under Review · Hidden · Sold / Rented · Drafts · Trash, plus type and sort. Below 1200 they collapse back into the mobile chip row and sheet.
  • Trash and Archived are this screen with a filter — same rows, restore instead of edit, and the retention line the app shows.
  • The empty state keeps the app's own "Create a listing" call.
