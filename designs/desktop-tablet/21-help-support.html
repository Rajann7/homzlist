Decisions

  • Search leads, categories follow as a carousel, then popular articles as a plain list — the order a person actually uses a help centre in.
  • An article gets a table of contents on the right at ≥1200. Help articles are the longest text in the product and the mobile screen has no way to skim them.
  • "Was this helpful?" keeps its place at the end of the article, and "Still need help?" leads straight into a new ticket with the article pre-attached.
  • Tickets become a split view — the list on the left, the thread on the right, with the app's own states (Replied · Closed) and its own empty lines ("No ticket is waiting on your reply.", "Every ticket you raised has been answered.").
  • Raise a grievance stays a distinct category, not a generic contact form — it is a compliance path and must remain findable.
